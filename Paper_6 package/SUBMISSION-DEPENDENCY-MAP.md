# Dependency map for the submission package

The article uses the credited OpenAI zero-free theorem. Its proof sources,
licences and matching historical audit records are supplied separately in
research/formal-proof-sources.zip. They are not part of the article TeX ZIP.

The article's mathematical proof and its attribution are unchanged in this
release. The verified closed formal endpoints use the supplied credited
theorem. An unrefereed source is not the same as an assumed conjecture;
source attribution and independent discovery are distinct questions.

The mean-square target in the research notes is an attempted independent
replacement and is not established. Classical Gauss/Jacobi identities and
finite parameter checks do not prove that analytic input. No stronger or
independent result is asserted by this submission-package consolidation.
